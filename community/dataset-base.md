# About Game

Hades' Star is a free-to-play online space strategy game where players build their own empires within a shared galaxy. You start with a single planet and gradually establish outposts, build a fleet, and research new technologies.

Key features:
- Co-op PvE missions [Red Stars](#red-star): work with other players to extract [resources](#artifacts) before the star goes supernova; aka RS or RSN -> RS10 = Red Star level 10

- PvP battles [Blue Stars](#blue-star): short matches played for rewards 1vs5; aka BLS

- Corporations or Corp: player alliances for large-scale missions in [White Stars](#white-star) e.g 10vs10; aka WS

- Stress-free progression: your empire's resources cannot be stolen while you are offline.

# Resources

The game features three key resources essential for developing your empire:

- Credits: The primary currency. Used to purchase ships and to build or upgrade infrastructure. The main way to earn them is by transporting cargo between colonies using transport ships. 

- Hydrogen (Hydro): Fuel. Consumed when moving ships between sectors and star systems, as well as for activating certain modules. Mined from asteroid fields using mining ships. 

- Crystals: Premium currency. Used to speed up construction and research, or to purchase Credits or Hydrogen. They can be earned through achievements and rare deliveries, or purchased with real money.

- AU (Arbitrary Unit): The game's unit of measurement for distance. It determines travel time and Hydrogen consumption when moving ships.
